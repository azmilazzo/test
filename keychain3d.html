How the outline is made

Your text is rasterized once, dilated with a round stroke (that’s the sticker border), and vectorized back into contours — no CSG kernel, no server, one closed solid per layer.

Print notes

Two overlapping shells (plate + letters) — slicers union them automatically, and you can pause for a filament swap at the letter layer. 0.2 mm layers, no supports.

Roadmap

Flower / star / heart ears, two-sided QR plate, articulated chain, 3MF with per-part colors. The template registry slot is ready for each.

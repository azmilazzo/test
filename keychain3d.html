Forged locally

Every glyph, boolean and facet is computed on your device with Three.js + BVH-CSG. No uploads, no accounts, no servers — the whole app is one HTML file.

Print notes

Binary STL in true millimetres with a real through-hole. PLA or PETG, 0.2 mm layers, no supports needed. Color isn’t stored in STL — choose filament in your slicer.

Roadmap

3MF export · luggage tags · desk signs · multi-line plaques. The template registry is already in place — new models plug in as new geometry builders.
